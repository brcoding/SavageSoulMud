
Beware the Archwraith!!

I have been traveling the lands looking for a
nice place to settle down in. Royal Oak seems like
an ideal place. Rusty has been kind enough to let
me stay for a very reasonable sum for the past month
while my family is on their way. Both Sue and Walt are
a great team and do a great job providing for everyone
in the community. It was because of them that I took
on my quest.

Near to the town there is an old set of ruins that
were once a library I believe. At night there were
spectres heading into the city from the ruins and
terrorizing everyone. I did some initial investigation
and found that the source was deep to the east in the
ruins. I started to look into the ruins during the day
while the light kept them at bay with my partner Reggie.
It wasn't long before we found a book lying on the
ground. The book was making a strange sound and at night
spectres would flow right into the world from the book.

It was at this point we decided to open the book. As
we turned the first page a force knocked me and Reggie
back. We lost our hearing and vision for a few minutes
and when we regained our senses we saw an unholy vision.
The wraith was staring into us, draining us. It was at this
moment it started to head towards the town. As we made
chase, the wraith headed for town and enabled the defense
systems on the outer wall. It went from person to person
stealing the key from everyone in town. It was at this
moment we realized what it was doing. We had no way out,
no way to escape!

I am sorry for what I have done, tonight I will again
go out and try to reduce the number of spectres and
clear a path for Reggie to make his attack. If we should
fail, use the mist dagger. the mist dagger is an
interdimensional tool that was found early on to provide
protection when the teleportation systems were having
"Issues". Just wield the dagger and attack the wraith.
