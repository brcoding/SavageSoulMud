Getting started
===============

New characters begin in Savsoul's Royal Oak Medical Room.
Type look to examine the room, then follow its listed exits. Directions work as commands: north, east, south, west, up, down and the four diagonals.

Useful first commands:
  score              Your Savage Soul attributes, XP and condition.
  train               Show attribute training choices.
  inventory           Items you carry.
  credits             Your money balance.
  help shops          Buying supplies and equipment.
  help combat         How fighting currently works.
  help topics         Browse the help library.
  commands            List the commands available to you.

New characters use Savage Soul rather than the old race/class picker. Existing characters keep their old identity metadata. Type quit when leaving.

Nurse Sue in the medical center hub (east of the starting room) gives you a Soulboy. For the Royal Oak escape quest, see help wraith.
