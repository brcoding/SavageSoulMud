Current implementation limits
=============================

Savsoul and several recovered areas are available, with placed NPCs and reconstructed shop stock. Some original rooms were incomplete placeholders. Recovered NPCs do not automatically regain all their old scripts or special behavior.

Savage Soul currently includes attribute training, six Peculiarities, task checks, melee duels, equipment and several conditions. The full tabletop system is not implemented: most Peculiarities/disadvantages, species templates, magic, ranged ammunition, tactical positioning, group combat, loot rewards and medical/natural wound healing remain pending.

Food and water can be consumed and save immediately; there is no hunger/thirst clock or automatic healing benefit.
The upstairs desk, mist dagger, Archwraith quest and south-gate passage are playable. The gate terminal offers a playable hacking override; most other recovered NPC scripts remain pending. Old race/class labels are identity metadata, not the new mechanical rules.
