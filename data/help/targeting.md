Choosing items and targets
==========================

Match an object's name or keywords. Full names are safest when several things match.
  get shovel          Take one matching shovel.
  get 2.shovel        Take the second matching shovel.
  get 3*shovel        Take up to three matching shovels.
  get all food        Take every matching food item.
  drop all            Drop all portable items you carry.
  give "water bottle" Rawr

Use 2.shovel rather than "second shovel": written ordinals are not selector syntax. Quoted phrases keep a multiword item together for give/steal.
Get, drop, buy and sell accept quantity/all selectors. Drink and eat consume one item at a time and accept numbered targets such as drink 2.bottle. Fixtures can be examined but cannot be picked up.
