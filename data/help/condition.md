Item condition

look <item>

An item's condition is shown in words, rather than a percentage:
excellent, good, worn, badly worn, or ruined.
Corpses use fresh, starting to decay, decaying, and badly decayed.
Their condition worsens until they rot away, one minute after death.

Condition belongs to each individual item and survives pickup, drop and saves.
Ordinary equipment does not yet lose condition automatically from use.
