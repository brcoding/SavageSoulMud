Savior of Royal Oak
===================

Nurse Sue in the medical center hub supplies your Soulboy. Keep it for the gate.

In the north upstairs rental room of Royal Oak Bar N Grill:
  disarm drawer
  open desk
  get all from desk
  read note
  wield dagger

From South Royal Oak Main Street (east of the medical center hub), go northeast onto the Faded Trail, then east nine times to ZombieMarsh07. Look for the disturbed earth and the Archwraith.
  attack wraith

Wielding the mist dagger banishes the wraith and consumes the dagger, granting Savior of Royal Oak, a metal gate key, 2000 XP and 10 quest points once per character. Ordinary weapons cannot defeat this apparition. The wraith returns after about a minute for other players; completion survives logout and reboot.

Return west nine times and southwest to Main Street, then south to the Wall Gate. With your Soulboy carried, open gate and go south to leave Royal Oak.
