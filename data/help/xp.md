XP and training costs
=====================

Total XP determines your training budget. Score shows total and available XP.
Ordinary attribute levels cost 50 XP times the new level; Body levels cost 100 XP times the new level. Training a single attribute cannot consume more than half your total XP.
Peculiarity capacity grows with total XP. Additional capacity can cost XP; learn checks the full available budget before changing your character.
New characters begin with 600 XP. Defeating creatures with authored XP rewards and completing rewarded quests awards XP. Combat reports earned XP at the end. A wizard can also grant XP using awardxp.
Exchange converts unspent XP to credits before you make any attribute or Peculiarity purchases. See help exchange.
