Savage Soul character rules
===========================

This MUD's character and combat mechanics use Savage Soul. New characters begin with 600 total XP and base human attributes.
  score               View attributes, XP, defenses, mana and conditions.
  train body          Buy the next level of an attribute.
  pecs                List implemented Peculiarities.
  learn quickness     Buy the next level of a Peculiarity.
  task perception 10  Make a task check against Difficulty Factor 10.

Trainable attributes: Body, Athletics, Craft, Defense, Intellect, Leadership, Melee Attack, Perception, Performance, Ranged Attack, Stealth and Will Power. Multiword names work with spaces or underscores.
Faith cannot be trained directly with XP, following the rulebook.
Only part of the complete tabletop rules is implemented. See help limitations.
