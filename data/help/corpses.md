Corpses

look corpse
get all from corpse
get corpse
drop corpse
get 2.corpse

Killed creatures leave a corpse in the room. It is an ordinary object you can
examine, carry and drop. They no longer act, trade or fight.
Any carried belongings fall beside the corpse; pick them up normally with get.
Corpses are not containers. Ordinary rats currently carry no loot.
Get-from requests report that the corpse is empty; belongings are on the ground.
Destroyed creatures leave remains; obliterated creatures leave no body.

Corpses rot away after one minute, including while carried or offline.
Look at a corpse to see its condition: fresh, starting to decay, decaying,
or badly decayed. Picking it up does not reset its lifetime.
Ground corpses also reset with the world at restart or copyover.

See also: help condition.
In Royal Oak Med Room, put corpse on table regenerates that creature.
Sit or lie on the table to heal yourself. See help medical-table.
