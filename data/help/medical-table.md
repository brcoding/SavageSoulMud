In Royal Oak Med Room, placing a carried corpse regenerates the creature
it belonged to. The table contains and scans it, then announces "Threat
detected" and vaporizes it after three seconds. This does not heal you.

Sit or lie on the table to restore your own wounds, fatigue and mana,
and remove poison from the Royal Oak desk. No corpse, XP or credits are needed.
Treatment is instant. You stay seated or lying until you use stand.
Stand before walking or attacking. Stand up works as well.
You must be able to act and out of combat. While processing a creature,
the table is busy. Decayed-away corpses cannot be used.
Corpses last one minute, including travel time.
