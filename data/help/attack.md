Attacking a creature
====================

attack <creature>
kill <creature>

Begin an automatic melee duel with a creature in your room. Rounds occur about every five seconds. Player-versus-player and group combat are unsupported. Use stop to end the duel. See help combat.

Attack stops at incapacitation; kill continues to death. Against ordinary rats more than two Sizes smaller than you, both commands select a trample instead of trying to punch a tiny target. See help trample.
