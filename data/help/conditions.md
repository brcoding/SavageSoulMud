Injury and other conditions
===========================

Score reports your current condition. Wounds accumulate from Uninjured through Barely Injured, Injured, Seriously Injured and Critically Injured, then Dead or worse. Injuries impose penalties.
Stunned combatants spend a later activation recovering. Outside combat, recover clears stun. Windedness can recover through rest and a Will Power check; rest does not heal wounds.
Unconsciousness and other incapacitating conditions prevent ordinary physical actions. Help and score remain available. Critical injuries can trigger later bleeding checks even after a fight ends.
Medical treatment and natural wound healing are not implemented yet. A wizard can use soulheal for recovery during development.
