Combat basics
=============

Attack a creature in your room to begin a duel:
  attack <creature>
  defend auto
  stop

Combat runs in roughly five-second rounds. Leadership determines activation order. Attacks and defenses use d10-based Savage Soul checks; an active Dodge uses your readiness for that round. Wield a carried weapon and wear carried armor before fighting.
Damage changes your injury condition rather than reducing a hit-point bar. Injury, stun, fatigue, windedness, armor and load affect checks. Critical wounds can worsen over time.
Current combat supports one player against one NPC. It has no PvP, group combat or ranged ammunition. Victory XP is shown at the end of combat. See help conditions, help defend and help limitations.

The Archwraith is a special quest encounter: ordinary weapons cannot harm it, and a wielded mist dagger resolves the original quest directly. See help wraith.

Ordinary rats remain Size 0, so normal strikes are difficult. Against these much smaller grounded creatures, attack/kill selects a trample: you move into their space, dealing Blunt damage equal to Mass at SM 0 unless they avoid it with a Desperation Dive. Hits knock them prone. They no longer have a forced flee timer. Use trample rat or stomp rat explicitly if desired. This is a room-scale adaptation; tactical facing and individual spaces are not simulated.

Attack stops when the opponent is incapacitated. Kill continues until the opponent is dead unless you stop, leave, or become incapacitated. A natural 10 continues with -5 + d10 when the initial normal attack total cannot reach Passive Defense.

Normal combat logs describe strikes, glancing contact and defenses. Brief mode keeps compact outcomes; detailed mode adds attack/defense totals and damage. Wording follows resolved results and does not add attacks or hit locations.
