These commands work on the trapped wooden desk in the upstairs north rental room of Royal Oak Bar N Grill. You can also get all from desk. Disarming this particular trap needs no tool or roll, matching the recovered source. Opening it while armed fires a one-use needle trap.

Its injected venom is adapted to Savage Soul: immediate Body versus DF 10, repeated every five seconds until resisted. Failure inflicts twice the failure margin as wound level; a critical failure kills. Disarm before opening. A wizard can use soulheal to clear venom and injuries.

Desk progress and its dagger/note are saved together per character. Dropping the items or reconnecting does not refill the desk. Wield dagger equips the recovered mist dagger. The note describes the wraith quest. See help wraith to complete it and earn passage through the south gate.
