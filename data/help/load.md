Carried load and equipment
==========================

Known Savage Soul equipment contributes its catalog weight to carried load. Score shows the load and any resulting condition or penalty. Excessive load can make you helpless or prevent movement; dropping items can reduce it.
Wield and wear select items already in your inventory. Equipment shows what is equipped. Remove clears a weapon, armor or both; it leaves the items in your inventory.
Imported items without a weight in the current equipment catalog contribute zero weight. This is a current conversion limitation.
