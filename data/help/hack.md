Hacking terminals

hack terminal
hack try CRANE
hack status
hack abort

Connect to a hackable terminal in your room. It lists eight five-letter
codes; one is correct. You have four attempts. Each wrong guess reports
how many letters are in the correct positions. For example, CRANE and
CRATE match in four positions. Compare feedback to eliminate codes.

Only listed codes count. Invalid or repeated guesses cost no attempts.
Exhausting attempts locks the device for 60 seconds, then allows a fresh
challenge. Abort disconnects but preserves your puzzle and remaining
attempts. Reconnecting or leaving the room does not reset them.
Return to the same terminal to continue. You cannot hack during combat
or while incapacitated. All feedback is plain text.

Success grants the terminal's configured access. Royal Oak's terminal
opens the city gate and completes Escape from Royal Oak, awarding
2000 XP and 10 quest points once. The gate still requires your Soulboy.
A solved terminal can apply its override again without further rewards.
