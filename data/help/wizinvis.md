Wizard only: wizinvis [on|off]

Toggle your invisibility, or choose on/off explicitly. Ordinary players will not
see you in rooms or player lists, and will not receive your action messages.
Other wizards can still see you. The preference survives normal saves and login.

Hidden staff cannot tell, give to, or steal from ordinary players. Turn visibility
off before interacting with them. External network commands are also unavailable
while hidden. Test accounts remain hidden regardless of this toggle.

This hides presence and messages; shared world changes still happen.
