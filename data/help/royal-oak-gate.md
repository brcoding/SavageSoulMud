The Royal Oak Wall Gate is south of South Royal Oak Main Street. Its south exit leads out of the city; the controls are west.

To earn passage, complete Savior of Royal Oak: recover the mist dagger from the trapped desk in the upstairs north inn room, wield it, and attack the Archwraith in the misty ruins. The dagger is consumed, and you receive the gate key, 2000 XP and 10 quest points once.

The gate also requires a carried Soulboy. Nurse Sue gives one free when you visit the medical center hub without one. Use activate soulboy to view status and quests. Return to the gate, open gate, then go south. Returning north has no quest requirement.

Alternatively, go west from the gate to the controls and use hack terminal. Solve its code puzzle to open the gate and complete Escape from Royal Oak. See help hack. The southern road and charging station are available; the Morlock Caves branch and procedural wasteland beyond the road are not imported yet. See help wraith for directions.
