Talking to trainers

Visit Old Man Tully in the small shack. Ask ordinary questions; you do not need
to include "for" or "about". The older ask tully for help still works.

ask tully how do I level up?
ask tully what should I spend my XP on?
ask tully how much does melee attack cost?
ask tully can I afford it?
talk to tully why is there a spending limit?
ask tully can I learn quickness?
ask tully how do I earn more XP?
ask tully what is an attribute?
ask tully about fatigue
ask tully for a list of peculiarities
ask tully about brutish

Tully checks your current character record. He can explain costs, limits,
Peculiarities, Combat capacity, Faith, exchanging XP for credits and respecs.
After discussing an attribute, "it" or "that" refers to that attribute.
Advice never buys anything. Use train <attribute> or learn <name> to purchase.
Named topics receive direct, brief answers. Ask about capacity for your remaining
Peculiarity levels, or costs for the full training formula.
When enabled, a tiny local model interprets other ordinary wording; the game itself
calculates and writes the answer. Scripted topics remain available if the model
is offline. Rephrase or ask for help if he does not understand. He is a trainer,
not a shopkeeper. You do not need to enable a mode or use special syntax.
