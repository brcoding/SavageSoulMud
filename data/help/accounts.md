Accounts and character saving
=============================

Log in using your account email and password, then select your character. An existing character name is also accepted at the login prompt. Follow the login prompts to register an account or create another character. Account login and character names are separate; keep the email you registered with.

Characters save on creation, movement and normal logout/disconnection. Shop transactions, consumables and Savage Soul character changes also save immediately. There is no regular timed autosave of every action; use quit for a clean departure.

Your saved character includes credits, inventory and Savage Soul state. Server copyover can preserve connected sessions under the current WSL service. A cold restart may require logging in again.
