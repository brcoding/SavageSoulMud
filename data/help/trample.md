trample rat
stomp rat

Ordinary rats are much smaller grounded creatures. Move into their space to trample instead of using a precision strike. The current room-scale implementation supports marked small creatures more than two Sizes smaller than you; you must be standing.

The target may avoid the attack with a Desperation Dive (Perception DF12). If it fails, apply Blunt damage equal to your Mass at SM0 and knock it prone. Normal Passive Defense and normal Dodge do not determine whether a trample hits. A ready creature can spend its activation to counterattack as you move in, before the trample. It gets no second attack that round. Reactions and knockdown consume any remaining readiness.

Attack/kill automatically chooses this action against ordinary rats. There is no forced rat retreat. Attack and explicit trample stop at incapacitation; kill continues to death. Use stop to break off.
