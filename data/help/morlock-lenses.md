# Morlock optical machine

In the Morlock machine room, use `look lenses` to inspect the four compass
bearings. `press red button`, `press blue button`, `press green button` and
`press yellow button` rotate different combinations of lenses. The map room's
technical diagram contains a clue.

Solving the machine opens a small panel and awards 100 XP and 10 quest points
once per character. Use `get suit from panel` or `get all from panel` to retrieve
its contents. The panel starts closing after 100 seconds and closes after two
minutes. Your quest reward and anything you take are saved.
