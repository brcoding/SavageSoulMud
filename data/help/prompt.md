Compact (default): [OK] >. Injury codes: BI barely injured, I injured,
SI seriously injured, CI critically injured. Flags: U unconscious, S stunned,
W winded, F fatigued. Dead, destroyed and obliterated are shown separately.
Status: condition, mana, total XP and credits.
Combat: condition and current opponent.
Classic: >. Accessible: Ready. Off: no prompt.

Custom prompts use 1-160 printable plain-text characters and these tokens:
{name} {condition} {xp} {credits} {mana} {maxmana} {opponent}
XP is total earned XP, not unspent XP. Unavailable values show a dash.
Screen reader mode uses Ready unless the prompt is off. Settings save immediately.
