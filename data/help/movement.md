Movement and exits
==================

Enter an exit direction to walk through it:
  north / n           east / e          south / s         west / w
  up / u              down / d
  northeast / ne      northwest / nw    southeast / se    southwest / sw

Look lists exits and doors. A closed door may need open <direction> first. Locked doors cannot currently be unlocked with a general unlock command.
Severe Savage Soul conditions and excessive carried load can prevent movement. See help conditions and help load.
Type map for a local map and areas for the area's directory.
