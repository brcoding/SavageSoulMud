Shops and credits
=================

Stand in a room with an active merchant, such as Trader Sam's Checkout.
  list                See stock, purchase prices and resale prices.
  buy water           Buy a water bottle.
  buy shovel          Buy a shovel.
  sell shovel         Sell a recognized item from your inventory.
  credits             See your current balance.

Credits are a single whole-number currency. Transactions save immediately. Merchants buy the item definitions in their stock; an empty bottle is not a full bottle and cannot be sold as one. Stock is currently unlimited.
Sam also sells Savage Soul weapons and armor. See help wield, help wear, help drink and help eat.
