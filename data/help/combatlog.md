combatlog brief
combatlog normal
combatlog detailed

Brief reports each round in one short line: hits, misses, wounds and recovery.
It leaves out initiative and roll calculations, so combat remains easy to follow.
Normal is the default: short hit and defense messages with the creature's name.
Avoided attacks describe dodges, footwork, and armed parries or blocks. Active
defense is Dodge; armed guard descriptions do not add another defense roll.
Detailed adds initiative, attack and defense totals, success margin and damage.
Screen reader mode always uses brief combat output.
Every fight ends with XP earned, including XP +0 when no reward was earned.
Rats award 5 XP once per defeat; finishing an incapacitated rat pays no second reward.
Other NPCs may have no reward. Quest rewards are separate.

See also: help combat, help screenreader, help prompt.
