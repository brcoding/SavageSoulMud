# Initial equipment catalog

All nine items use Average quality (quality rating 1). Appendix B.1 states that Buyer’s Guide listed costs already represent Average quality; no multiplier applies. Armor is Size 5 and must fit the wearer. Its `min_size: 5` represents that catalog size, not permission for larger wearers to use it. Size-based catalog variants are not included yet.

The source names “Knife, Combat,” “Knife, Pocket,” “Sword, Light,” “Sword, Heavy,” “Club, Light (Org.),” and “Club, Heavy (Org.)” are displayed in natural command order. The clubs are organic and cause blunt damage. Both knives cause sharp stabbing damage, with no attack bonus. Both swords cause sharp slashing damage and grant +1 attack.

`damage_modifier` is added to the wielder’s Strength, not a standalone DC. The organic light/heavy club modifiers are +0/+1; pocket/combat knife -1/+0; light/heavy sword +1/+2. Sources: rulebook §7B.6, Appendix B.4 and C.1.11–12 (PDF pages 365–366 and 383).

Armor bonuses increase Body-based DR; sharp armor bonuses increase Body-minus-2 Sharp DR. They are +1/+2, +2/+4 and +3/+6 for light, medium and heavy. Average coverage is 6; an attack’s success margin must exceed coverage to bypass armor. Sources: rulebook §7.4.3 and Appendix B.7 (PDF page 371).

Costs and weights: combat knife 200 credits/0.5 kg; pocket knife 100/0.25; light sword 400/1; heavy sword 600/2; light club 50/1; heavy club 100/2; light armor 500/5; medium armor 1,000/15; heavy armor 2,000/35. Sell values are 50% of retail rounded down. That percentage is an explicit local MUD merchant policy, not a Savage Soul rule.

## Workbook differences

Soulgen “Buyers Guide” F:O agrees on prices and most weights/modifiers. Its organic clubs have DC values 1 and 2 instead of the rulebook’s 0 and 1; its light sword/light club minimum sizes are 3 instead of 2, and pocket knife minimum size is 1 instead of 0. This catalog follows the rulebook’s explicitly labeled weapon table. The workbook’s armor-specific row 195 agrees on heavy armor weight 35 kg, while its general catalog row 420 says 40 kg. This catalog follows the dedicated armor table and the rulebook.
